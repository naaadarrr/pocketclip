# Privacy Policy — Pocketclip

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Pocketclip is a Chrome extension that captures screenshots of the page you are viewing and lets you annotate them locally.

## Data we collect
We collect **nothing**.

- No accounts, analytics, or tracking
- No servers: screenshots stay on your device
- Images are stored only in Chrome local extension storage / memory for editing, then cleared when you leave or capture again
- We do not upload screenshots, page content, or personal information

## Permissions
- **activeTab** / **scripting**: used only when you click a capture action, to screenshot the current tab
- **storage**: temporary local storage for the capture being edited

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing support email.
